SAMPLE ITINERARY

Your Kerala Journey

A carefully planned journey covering some of Kerala's most beautiful destinations.

DAY 01

Kochi → Munnar

Arrival at Kochi and transfer towards the beautiful hill station of Munnar. Enjoy scenic views, tea plantations and the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

DAY 02

Explore Munnar

Discover the natural beauty of Munnar with sightseeing around the tea gardens, viewpoints and popular attractions.

DAY 03

Thekkady → Alappuzha

Continue the journey through Kerala's scenic landscapes before reaching Alappuzha, famous for its peaceful backwaters.

DAY 04

Alappuzha → Kochi

Enjoy the backwater experience and proceed towards Kochi for departure or onward travel.
